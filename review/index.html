Review the test questions

MARCmin's first experiment asks small search models to find the right book for 120 patron-style questions. An AI assistant wrote those questions while reading each book's record. Before any result is published, a librarian needs to judge them.

For each question you see the book it was written for. Answer two quick questions, and suggest better wording if you like. One subject area is 24 questions, about 15 minutes. Your answers stay in this browser until you send them.
